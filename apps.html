Apps

Built by Glen Rogers · Be Strong Again · Wisconsin

I own a gym and coach for a living. I started writing software because the tool I needed did not exist and the person who needed it was standing in front of me.

That is still how every one of these got made. A parent in the bleachers who wanted the scores to still be there in March. A coach running a contest in a basement with no signal. A friend with a trucking company and a whiteboard. None of them were market research. All of them were somebody I could name.

Most of these are free, and the ones that are free will stay free.

Why I do this

I have a wife, kids, and a gym full of people, and nearly everything here started with one of them needing something that did not exist.

Some of it is for my own family — a game the kids can play four at a time, a way to keep a gymnast's scores so they are still there in March, a list of what is in a cabin so we stop forgetting. Some of it is for people who would never have had software written for them otherwise. A friend with a trucking company. A trainer keeping her business in a spreadsheet. A church.

And some of it is because these tools landed in my hands at a strange moment in history, and I would rather aim them at something good than at something that sells. I am not a programmer by trade. I coach. But I can describe a problem clearly and I am stubborn about finishing, and it turns out that is most of the job now.

Two things I care about: helping people get strong, and building something that outlasts me. Nearly everything on this page is one or the other.

On the App Store

The Week Ender

A coach's Friday text message, made into an app. Answer three questions on Thursday night, get an hour-by-hour plan for the weekend on Friday morning. A rough week earns a looser plan, not a stricter one.

On the App Store

Cabin Notes

For people who own a seasonal cabin they do not rent out. Photo inventory, a running bring-list, the closing and opening checklist, and a guest guide you can text. Every part of it works in airplane mode.

On the App Store

Footsteps of the Teacher

Kids learn to write real code by building the Bible stories they already know by heart. Type a line, the donkey appears. Ada the owl catches typos, guesses what you meant, and never scolds.

In review

Strongman Contest

Run a contest from one phone with no internet, because gyms are basements. Place-point scoring done properly — an event win is worth N points for N entrants, ties split the pooled slots, and holds sort the opposite way to medleys.

Submitted

Season Book

A gymnast's meet scores across a whole season, on a parent's own iCloud. Women's four apparatus or men's six, personal bests, and a wall display for the gym television.

Submitted

Being built

Two or Three

One topic, everyone at the table, and each person asked the version of the question that fits where they actually are — a six-year-old and a fourteen-year-old get different questions about the same thing. Ten minutes, then the phone goes face down. Free, with no ads and nothing to buy.

If you need something built

I build software for people and causes I believe in, and I usually do it for nothing. Churches, coaches, small operators, families — anybody who needs one good tool and cannot justify paying somebody to make it. If it is about fitness or about leaving something behind, I am especially interested.

I take on a few of these a year. I would rather finish three things properly than start ten. If that sounds like what you need, write to me and tell me who it is for.
